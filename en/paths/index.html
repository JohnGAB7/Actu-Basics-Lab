A shared progression

From foundations to expertise

We first build mathematical fluency, data literacy and sound reasoning, then learn to select methods, implement them and defend our results.

01Foundations · Understand

Calculate and represent

Fractions, ratios, percentages, powers, algebra, functions, notation, charts and units. Read a problem, check an order of magnitude and explain what a number represents.

Variables, populations, samples, description, visualisation, probability, distributions, dependence, estimation and uncertainty. Turn a real question into a statistical question.

Model and decide

Regression, tests, statistical models, time series, econometrics, simulation and validation. Select a suitable method, assess its limitations and interpret its results.

Learn by doing

Four tools, four complementary roles

Tools will be introduced when they add real value. Concepts will not be artificially repeated in four languages: we choose the relevant tool, then learn to transfer methods between environments.

01 · Spreadsheet

Excel

Formulas, references, tables, sorting, filters, charts, statistical functions, pivot tables, controls and scenarios. Build clear, auditable analyses.

From basic calculations to business analysis
02 · Automation

Excel VBA

Variables, conditions, loops, procedures, functions, Excel objects, report automation and repeatable controls. Introduced when repetition or scale justifies code.

Automate without making processes opaque
03 · General-purpose programming

Python

Syntax, functions, data structures, notebooks, pandas, visualisation, simulation, APIs, pipelines, machine learning and gradual production practices.

From analysis to reproducible workflows
04 · Statistics and research

R

Data wrangling, visualisation, descriptive and inferential statistics, models, simulation, reproducible reports and specialised methods.

Deep practice in statistical reasoning
