3 out of 12: what percentage is that?

In a team of twelve people, three cycle to work. What percentage is that? No calculator: simplify the fraction, convert to a decimal, then to a percentage.

Learning objectives

  • Simplify a fraction.
  • Convert a fraction to a decimal and then a percentage.
  • Distinguish a ratio (part to part) from a fraction (part to whole).
  • Apply the rule of three in a proportional situation.

1. Simplifying a fraction

To simplify a fraction, divide the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.

3 / 12 = (3 ÷ 3) / (12 ÷ 3) = 1 / 4

Three twelfths and one quarter represent the same share: only the notation changes.

2. Fraction, decimal, percentage

1 / 4 = 0.25 = 25 / 100 = 25%

Useful reference fractions: 1/2 = 50%, 1/4 = 25%, 1/5 = 20%, 1/10 = 10%.

3. Ratio or fraction?

In the team, 3 people cycle and 9 use another way to get to work.

Ratio cyclists / others = 3 / 9 = 1 / 3
Fraction of cyclists in the group = 3 / 12 = 1 / 4 = 25%

A ratio compares one part with another part. A fraction compares a part with the whole. Reading “1 to 3” as “one third of the group” would be wrong: the group has 1 + 3 = 4 parts.

4. The rule of three

A recipe needs 300 g of flour for 4 people. How much is needed for 6 people, assuming the quantity is proportional to the number of guests?

For 1 person: 300 ÷ 4 = 75 g
For 6 people: 75 × 6 = 450 g
The rule of three only works when the situation is proportional. Oven preheating time, for example, does not double when the number of guests doubles.

Out of 40 pupils, 6 have a bus pass. What percentage is that?
