Our intention

Awaken curiosity.
Make numbers understandable.

ACTU-BASICS is an independent educational project dedicated to statistics, probability, econometrics and actuarial science.

Our promise

Every concept starts with a question, builds intuition, introduces an explicit method and lets learners check what they have understood. Mathematics and statistics are not a collection of formulas: they are tools for reasoning about the world.

Rigor before simplification

An accessible explanation must not erase assumptions, units, limitations or uncertainty.

Understand before memorising

Start with a concrete example, explain why the method works, then generalise.

Transparent sources

Facts, data, fictional examples and assumptions must be identifiable.

Bilingual by design

French and English editions remain consistent in numbers and reasoning while reading naturally in each language.

A four-step learning method

01

Question

A concrete situation creates a reason to learn.

02

Intuition

Build meaning before formalisation.

03

Formalise

Definitions, formulas, assumptions and limitations become explicit.

04

Practise

An exercise checks understanding and transfer.

An independent laboratory

This website is developed separately from the reference repository. Content is integrated progressively, with a clear distinction between published lessons, source scripts and units still in the curriculum. The original repository remains preserved.
